About Highway Patrol

The premier law enforcement agency of the state, the San Andreas State Police Highway Patrol Division is responsible for regulating traffic, enforcing state statutes, and providing critical emergency support across all highways and rural corridors.

Whether conducting high-speed interdictions, state-wide tactical pursuits, or assisting local municipal jurisdictions, State Troopers uphold the highest standard of professionalism across San Andreas.

What We Do

Highway Interdiction

Patrolling state highways and freeways to manage traffic flow, conduct interdictions, and reduce motor vehicle fatalities.

Tactical Operations

Deploying specialized tactics and high-risk response units for barricaded suspects, warrant executions, and state crises.

Commercial Enforcement

Inspecting commercial transport vehicles to ensure safety compliance and prevent contraband trafficking across county lines.

State Investigation

Assisting local law enforcement with high-level criminal investigations, officer-involved incidents, and major reconstruction.
