About District Zero

The premier law enforcement agency of the state, the District Zero Highway Patrol Division is responsible for regulating traffic, enforcing state statutes, and providing critical emergency support across all highways and rural corridors.

Whether conducting high-speed interdictions, state-wide tactical pursuits, or assisting local municipal jurisdictions, State Troopers uphold the highest standard of professionalism across District Zero.
