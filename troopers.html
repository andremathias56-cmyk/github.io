Highway Patrol

"Obedience to the Law is Liberty"

Division Overview

The San Andreas State Police Highway Patrol Division serves as the primary law enforcement agency for state highways, freeways, and intercounty traffic corridors across San Andreas.

Our Troopers are trained in high-speed pursuit intervention, criminal interdiction, collision investigation, and providing primary or secondary emergency support to local municipal agencies.

Trooper Duties

Traffic Enforcement

Maintaining safety across highways through speed enforcement, DUI checkpoints, and proactive traffic stops.

State Interdiction

Intercepting illicit narcotics, illegal firearm trafficking, and high-value contraband traveling state routes.

Pursuit Intervention

Executing PIT maneuvers, tire-deflation deployment, and tactical vehicle pursuits safely across state roads.
