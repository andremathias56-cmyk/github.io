Patrol Division

About Patrol

The backbone of the San Andreas State Police, our Patrol Division is dedicated to maintaining safety, enforcing laws, and providing swift responses to emergencies. Whether you're cruising through state highways or responding to critical incidents, patrol officers play a vital role in ensuring the community thrives.

What We Do

  • Traffic Enforcement: Ensure road safety by conducting routine traffic stops, handling accidents, and addressing violations.
  • Emergency Response: Be the first on the scene to provide assistance during emergencies, crimes in progress, or public disturbances.
  • Proactive Policing: Conduct routine patrols, identify potential threats, and build rapport with the community to prevent crime before it happens.
  • Incident Reporting: Document incidents thoroughly to ensure accurate records and support further investigations.

Patrol Certifications

Disclaimer - The following certifications listed are available but not limited to.

State Officer Standards and Training (POST)

This certification is mandatory for all full-time law enforcement officers. It encompasses comprehensive training in laws, legal procedures, defensive tactics, firearms proficiency, and community policing.

Field Training Officer (FTO) Program

The Field Training Officer Program is an intensive, hands-on training process designed to bridge the gap between academy learning and real-world policing.

Traffic Unit (SASP-TU)

The Traffic Unit assembles highly trained and skilled automobile and motorbike operators to monitor traffic issues across the state.
